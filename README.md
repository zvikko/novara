# Novara Horizons Creations — Portfolio Showcase

**Custom PHP and JavaScript business systems for clinics, restaurants, shops, and service businesses.**

Live showcase: [zvikko.github.io/novara](https://zvikko.github.io/novara)

---

## About

Novara Horizons Creations designs and deploys production-ready operational systems tailored to specific industry workflows. Each system is built from scratch using PHP, JavaScript, and MySQL, then hosted and maintained on self-managed VPS infrastructure.

---

## Systems in This Portfolio

### Clinic System v2 — Healthcare
Full clinic operations platform covering admissions, vitals, pharmacy dispensing, lab workflows, billing, and claims processing.

**Modules:** Admissions · Vitals · Pharmacy · Lab Results · Billing

### Restaurant App — Hospitality
Point of sale, kitchen coordination, stock management, and fiscal day workflows for food service operations.

**Modules:** POS · Kitchen · Stock · Fiscal Reports · Branches

### Shop App — Retail
Sales, accounting, stock control, and staff attendance for single and multi-branch retail operations.

**Modules:** Sales · Stock · Accounting · Attendance · Multi-branch

### Novara Demo Portal — Platform
Lead capture, request validation, and guided demo provisioning system for controlled product trials.

**Modules:** Lead Capture · Request Validation · Admin Review · Demo Provisioning
